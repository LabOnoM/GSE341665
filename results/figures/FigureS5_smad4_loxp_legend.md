# Supplementary Figure S5 — legend

**RNA sequencing identifies the floxed *Smad4* allele but cannot determine whether it
was excised.** It shows what the reads establish and why they cannot settle excision or its
efficiency.

Six bulk tooth-germ libraries: *n* = 3 dcKO (K14-creER (tamoxifen-inducible)) versus 3
Cre-negative Smad4^fl/fl^ littermate controls. GRCm39 / Ensembl release 116, chr18; *Smad4* is on
the **minus strand**, so transcription runs right → left in A–C.

**(A)** Locus schematic. The floxed exon (black) is
**chr18:73,782,797–73,782,980, 184 bp**; triangles are
the loxP insertion points, site A
73,782,242/73,782,243 (±3 bp) and
site B 73,784,709/73,784,710 (exact);
shading is the 2,467 bp floxed interval. Dashed = the exon
8 → exon 10 junction Cre would create, never observed.

**(B)** All 15 reads carrying the 34 bp loxP core, in
3/3 CON and 3/3 dcKO libraries (solid = aligned, open =
soft-clipped, black = core; ‡ = a duplicate read, so 14 independent fragments). loxP reads are expected in Cre-negative CON, which carries
the identical allele; both cassettes are intronic, so read number is uninformative
about excision. Below: the reference sequence at each insertion point, and one cassette-spanning
read (CON1, `70S79M` at 73,784,710) in literal base calls.

**(C)** Per-base coverage across the locus, and for each of the 12 canonical exons the
dcKO/CON ratio on both normalisations — per-base **depth** and exonic **reads**, with each
library plotted. All exons lie within 0.889–1.094; the floxed exon is
0.924 on depth (*P* = 0.10) and
0.889 on reads
(*P* = 0.10), and the metrics rank the exons differently.

**(D)** Uniquely mapped reads on the two flanking junctions and on the predicted excision
junction
(chr18:73,782,125–73,790,265),
one bar per library. Per-exon *P* values are in `output/smad4_exon_ratio.csv`. The Cre excision
is not detected: **0 reads, 6/6
libraries**.

**The limit.** The allele is present; the excision product is undetectable — 0 skip reads
in 6/6 libraries against 121–187 reads on each flanking junction, PSI =
1.000 everywhere, one-sided 95% bound (rule of three)
≥ 99.4% of dcKO *Smad4* mRNA
retaining the exon. Coverage cannot reach significance either:
*P* = 0.10 is the floor of the exact
20-permutation test at *n* = 3 vs 3. Both coverage and
junctions are measured on **surviving mRNA**, so neither can distinguish little recombination
from recombination followed by decay of the frameshifted transcript. Deciding excision and its
efficiency requires a DNA- or protein-level assay: genomic PCR across the floxed interval, an
amplicon spanning the excision junction, or SMAD4 immunostaining.
